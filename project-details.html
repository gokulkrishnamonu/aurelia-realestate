Aurora Residences

A calm urban address shaped around light, landscape and generous shared spaces.

The idea

A quieter form of city living.

Aurora is planned as a low-density residential environment with daylight-first apartments, planted thresholds and amenities that feel integrated rather than ornamental.

Every residence balances privacy with visual connection to the landscape. Natural materials and restrained detailing keep the architecture timeless.

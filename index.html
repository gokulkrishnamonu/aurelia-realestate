Frequently asked questions

Questions buyers often ask.

What types of properties does Aurelia Developments build?

Our portfolio covers premium apartments, private villas and commercial developments. Individual project pages provide configuration, area, location and status details.

How can I get detailed specifications for a project?

Contact our team through the enquiry page. We can provide the relevant project information, specifications, availability and supporting documentation.

Where are your projects located?

Our sample portfolio on this website is focused on Kochi, Kakkanad and Ernakulam. Project-specific pages should be checked for exact location and current availability.

Can I arrange a site visit?

Yes. Contact the sales team to request a site visit and discuss the project, configuration and current availability.

Do you provide post-handover support?

Aftercare is treated as part of the customer experience. The exact scope and process depend on the project and its applicable handover terms.

How do I verify project information?

Use the relevant project page and request the official project documentation from our team. Buyers should independently review applicable approvals, agreements, specifications and statutory information before purchase.
